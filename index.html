questions

What's in a pocket?

New, unworn last-of-the-season pieces from one brand. A tops pocket mixes tops, dresses, knits and layers in one letter size; a bottoms pocket holds pants, skirts or denim in your number size. The number of pieces depends on the brand, and every pocket is worth about three times its member price.

Do I see what's inside before I buy?

No. You'll know the brand, the pocket price and that everything is in your size. The pieces themselves are the surprise.

How much does a box cost?

It depends on the brand and what's inside, but pocket prices are always low: a fraction of what the pieces would cost at full price. Each brand's price is announced with its drop, and waitlist members hear first.

Can I return a box?

Boxes are final sale, like sample sales. To make sure it fits, every pocket is packed to the size you choose before you buy.

Where do you deliver?

Pickup in New York City on drop weekend, or shipping anywhere in the US.

Where do the pieces come from?

Directly from the brands. Every season leaves pieces behind, and instead of sending them elsewhere, brands pack them into boxes for people who already love what they make.

Why are you doing this?

Fashion makes more than it sells. Industry estimates put unsold stock at 20 to 30% of every season, and in the US about 11.3 million tons of textiles went to landfill in a single year, with only about 15% recycled (EPA). Unsold clothes often end up with liquidators, in landfills or burned. The EU now bans large brands from destroying unsold clothes. We think those pieces deserve a better ending: new, unworn clothes go to members who will actually wear them.
